## Why SAP Business One users need Conversational BI

Distributors, manufacturers and retailers running SAP Business One make decisions on stock, credit, pricing and margins every day. After all, their ERP already holds this information. However, getting a clear answer out of it usually means:

- **Searching reports:** the figure is spread across several standard reports, so you first need to know which one to open.
- **Applying filters:** you choose the right date ranges, customers, items and warehouses, and every selection has to be right.
- **Exporting to Excel:** you rework the data in a spreadsheet that soon goes out of date.
- **Waiting for someone else:** when no report fits, the question goes to a colleague or consultant, and the decision waits too.

Conversational BI changes this. Instead of learning where the data lives, users simply ask what they want to know.

## How Conversational BI works in Clarior

1. **Ask naturally.** First, type a business question in everyday language.
2. **Clarior interprets it.** Next, Clarior uses AI to understand the request and prepare an authorized, read-only database query.
3. **Explore the results.** Finally, see the answer as a table or chart, ask follow-up questions, and save useful insights to dashboards.

For example, business users can ask Clarior:

- "Who are my top 10 customers this year?"
- "Which customers have overdue payments?"
- "Which products haven't moved in the last 90 days?"
- "How did our sales perform last month?"
- "Which products generate the highest margins?"

And many more.

## Built for secure Conversational BI on SAP Business One

Clarior lets business users ask freely, while their ERP data stays protected:

- **Read-only queries:** Clarior cannot change any data in SAP Business One.
- **Customer-hosted gateway:** Clarior connects to SAP Business One through a gateway in the customer's own environment. In addition, configured permissions control what each user can access.
- **Transactional data stays with the customer:** query results remain in the customer's environment, so Clarior does not send them to the cloud AI service. The cloud services only handle the question text, conversation context, authorized schema information and certain metadata.
