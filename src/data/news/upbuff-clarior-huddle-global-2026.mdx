**Kovalam, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India, November 2026.** UpBuff Technologies, the company behind the AI-powered ERP Execution Platform, took part in **Huddle Global 2026**, held from **November 12 to 14, 2026** at Kovalam. At **stall H15**, the UpBuff team showcased **[Clarior, Conversational BI for SAP Business One](/products/clarior)**, just days after its global launch on November 8.

Over three days, founders, business owners, ERP users, investors and speakers stopped by the stall. They asked Clarior real business questions in plain language and watched the answers appear as tables and charts, with no reports, filters or Excel exports.

## About Huddle Global 2026

Huddle Global is the annual startup festival organised by **Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM)**. It brings startups, investors, mentors, corporates and policy makers together by the beach at Kovalam. The 2026 edition ran for three days, with startup exhibitions, panel discussions and investor sessions, including a well-attended panel titled *"The VC Truth: Build What Matters, Not What Gets Funded."*

For UpBuff, a Technopark-based company, Huddle Global 2026 was a chance to show a home-grown AI product to Kerala's startup community and to visitors from India and abroad.

## The Clarior Live Challenge at stall H15

The idea at the stall was simple: **bring a business question and see Clarior answer it.** Visitors typed their questions in everyday language, and Clarior answered them from a demonstration SAP Business One database. Typical questions included:

- "Who are my top 10 customers this year?"
- "Which customers have overdue payments?"
- "Which products haven't moved in the last 90 days?"
- "How did our sales perform last month?"

Each answer came back as a table or chart within seconds. Visitors could then ask a follow-up question, just as they would ask a colleague. Many conversations at the stall also turned to data security. The team explained how Clarior keeps ERP data protected: queries are read-only, access follows each user's SAP Business One authorizations, and query results stay in the customer's own environment.

Visitors also took home the Clarior flyer with its message: **Stop searching. Start asking.**
