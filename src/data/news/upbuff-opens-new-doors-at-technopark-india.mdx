**Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India, September 30, 2026.** UpBuff, the company behind the AI-powered ERP Execution Platform, on Wednesday opened the doors to its new home in the **Nila building at Technopark Phase 1, Thiruvananthapuram**, marking a new beginning for the company. **Shiburaj Krishnapillai, CEO and Founder of UpBuff**, and **Rajesh Kattilepurath, Co-founder and CMO**, inaugurated the new office in the presence of the UpBuff team.

The move gives UpBuff's product, engineering and ERP consulting teams a bigger home under one roof. It is where the next chapter of UpBuff's story will be written, for customers in India and abroad.

## A new beginning in Kerala's largest IT park

Technopark in Thiruvananthapuram is one of India's leading IT parks and home to hundreds of technology companies. UpBuff has been part of the Technopark community, and its new home in the Nila building, Phase 1, marks the start of a new chapter there. From Nila, UpBuff stays close to Kerala's technology talent and partner companies.

## A home for a lively, diverse team

At UpBuff, culture is not a poster on the wall. It is in how the team works every day. Engineers, ERP consultants, designers and customer success specialists from different backgrounds, regions and experiences work side by side. They share ideas openly and celebrate wins together.

The new office was designed around that culture. It brings the whole team under one roof, with open spaces for collaboration, learning and the festivals and celebrations that make UpBuff feel like a second home. It is a workplace where fresh graduates and experienced professionals learn from each other, and where every voice helps shape the product.

## Built for the next stage of growth

UpBuff helps businesses run day-to-day operations on top of their ERP without changing the ERP core. Its platform connects directly with **SAP Business One, SAP S/4HANA, Oracle ERP and Epicor ERP**. It covers sales and distribution, field service, warehouse and inventory, manufacturing and shop floor, CRM, partner portals, product configuration, asset tracking, and AI-driven PO-to-SO and invoice automation.

UpBuff's solutions are built to help businesses meet local tax, e-invoicing and regulatory compliance requirements in every region they operate in. The new office will be the base for:

- **Product engineering:** building and improving UpBuff's ERP-integrated apps and AI automation.
- **ERP implementation and support:** SAP Business One and SAP S/4HANA integration projects for customers in India, the Middle East and Africa.
- **Customer success:** onboarding, training and day-to-day support for UpBuff customers.
