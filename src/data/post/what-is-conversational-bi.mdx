**Conversational BI (conversational business intelligence) lets you ask questions about your business data in everyday language and get the answers back as tables, charts and dashboards.** Instead of opening report screens, setting filters or waiting for someone to build a report, you simply type a question such as "Which customers have overdue payments?" and see the answer in seconds.

For SAP Business One companies, the data is already in the ERP. The hard part is getting a clear answer out of it. This guide explains how conversational BI works, how it compares with traditional BI, AI chatbots and other conversational BI tools, and what to check before you choose one. We use [Clarior](/products/clarior), UpBuff's conversational BI for SAP Business One, as the running example.

## What is conversational BI?

Business intelligence (BI) turns business data into information people can act on, traditionally through reports and dashboards built by specialists. Conversational BI keeps that goal but lets you talk to your data in plain language, the way you would ask a colleague. For example, in Clarior:

- **You ask:** "How did our sales perform last month compared with the month before?"
- **Clarior answers:** a short table of both months, the difference, and a chart you can save to a dashboard.

You can then ask a follow-up, such as "Break that down by region", without starting from scratch.

## How it works, step by step

Most tools follow the same five steps:

1. **You ask a question in everyday language.** No query language, no report menus.
2. **AI interprets the question.** It works out which data, time period, filters and measures you mean.
3. **The system prepares a database query.** It uses your question and your database structure (which tables and fields exist, not your records). In Clarior, the cloud AI prepares an authorized, read-only query without seeing your records.
4. **The query runs and you see the answer.** The result comes back as a table or chart. In Clarior, the query runs inside your own environment, so your records stay with you.
5. **You explore further.** Ask follow-up questions, change the view, or save the answer to a dashboard.

## Conversational BI vs traditional BI

Traditional BI is not going away: formal reports and well-designed dashboards still have their place. By traditional BI here, we mean fixed ERP reports, pre-built dashboards and the queue for custom reports. Users can already filter pre-built dashboards, and Power BI and SAP Analytics Cloud now add natural-language features too (see below). The difference is the brand-new question that no report or data model covers yet.

| Aspect | Traditional BI | Conversational BI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How you get an answer | Open a report or dashboard, set filters | Type a question in plain language |
| Who can answer a new question | People who can build or change reports and data models | Anyone who can describe what they need |
| New questions | Often need a new report or data model built by IT or a consultant | Ask straight away |
| Follow-up questions | Re-run the report with new filters | Ask a follow-up in the same conversation |
| Best for | Fixed, repeated reporting and formal outputs | Everyday questions and quick exploration |

## Conversational BI vs a general AI chatbot

It is easy to confuse conversational BI with a general-purpose AI chatbot such as ChatGPT. Used on its own, a chatbot answers from its training and from what you paste or upload. It doesn't know your sales figures or customer balances. Some chatbots can be connected to business systems through plugins or connectors such as MCP, but then you set up the connection, permissions and data protection yourself. We cover those routes in our guide on [how to connect AI to SAP Business One](/blog/how-to-connect-ai-to-sap-business-one).

Conversational BI is connected to your business data, so its answers come from a query against your system. For ERP data, a well-designed tool also respects user permissions, never changes your data, and keeps your records and query results inside your environment.

## Why it matters for SAP Business One users

SAP Business One holds accurate data, yet many teams still struggle to get quick answers from it. We see four problems again and again:

- **Decisions on old numbers.** By the time a report is run, exported and shared, stock, receivables and sales have already moved on.
- **A growing report backlog.** Every new question becomes a request to IT or a consultant.
- **Numbers that don't match.** Each team builds its own spreadsheet from its own export, so figures differ between departments.
- **Only experts get answers.** Owners and managers wait for the few people trained on SAP Business One reports.

Conversational BI fixes the first, second and fourth directly. Matching numbers needs one more step, because the same question can be calculated in different ways: "sales" can mean orders, deliveries or invoices, with or without credit memos, and "overdue" can count from the due date or the posting date. Agree what each measure means, check the answer once against your standard SAP Business One report, and save it as a dashboard. In Clarior, a saved dashboard keeps its query, so every refresh runs the same agreed calculation.

## What to check before choosing a tool for your ERP

Not every tool that "chats with your data" is ready for ERP data. Check these points:

1. **Is it read-only?** Asking a question should never be able to change records in your ERP. Ask how this is enforced: the strongest guarantee is a read-only database account, because the database itself blocks any change, even if someone tries to trick the AI with a cleverly worded question.
2. **Where does your data go?** Find out which data stays in your environment and which data is sent to cloud AI services.
3. **Does it follow your permissions?** A sales user and a finance manager asking the same question should see different answers if their access is different.
4. **Is it built for your ERP?** SAP Business One uses its own tables and codes, such as OINV for A/R invoices and OCRD for business partners. A tool built around that model starts from a better place, but ask each vendor how they test accuracy, and try it with your own questions.
5. **Can you check the answers?** You should be able to verify results against your standard reports, especially in the first weeks.
6. **Can you save results?** Useful answers should be easy to keep as a dashboard.

## The limits of conversational BI

Conversational BI is powerful, but it has limits:

- **Clear questions get better answers.** A specific question such as "Sales last month by region" works better than a vague one such as "How are we doing?" Good tools ask for clarification when a question is vague.
- **Answers are only as good as your data.** If items are posted late or master data is messy, the answers will reflect that.
- **It does not replace formal reporting.** Statutory reports, audits and month-end packs still belong in your standard reporting process.
- **Verify early on.** When we test conversational BI with SAP Business One, we check its answers against SAP Business One's standard reports. We recommend you do the same during your first weeks.

## Other ways to get conversational BI on SAP Business One

Several tools already let you ask SAP Business One questions in plain language. The core techniques, such as turning questions into database queries (text-to-SQL) and connecting through a gateway inside your network, are well established. The options differ in setup, where your data must live first, and security:

- **SAP Analytics Cloud natural language query ("Just Ask").** Ask questions in plain language about data that has first been connected and modelled in SAP Analytics Cloud.
- **Microsoft Power BI with Q&A and Copilot.** Natural-language features on Power BI data models built from SAP Business One data, with Microsoft's on-premises data gateway reaching local databases. Suits teams with Power BI skills in-house.
- **SAP Business One add-ons.** For example, the Boyum B1UP AI Assistant turns plain-language questions into SQL and shows the results in a table, and Vision33's Copilot for SAP Business One, built on the Saltbox integration platform, handles questions and guided processes.
- **Build your own.** Open-source frameworks such as Vanna are the most flexible route, but your team designs and maintains the security, permissions and data masking.

If you already run Power BI or SAP Analytics Cloud, their natural-language features may suit you. If you want answers straight from SAP Business One without first building a BI project, read on.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does conversational BI mean?**
Using everyday language to ask questions about your business data, and getting the answers as tables, charts or dashboards.

**Is conversational BI the same as conversational analytics?**
Almost. Both mean asking questions of your data in natural language; "conversational BI" leans more toward business reporting and dashboards.

**Do I need to know SQL or reporting tools to use conversational BI?**
No. The AI turns your question into a database query for you.

**Can conversational BI change data in SAP Business One?**
It shouldn't. Choose a read-only tool for ERP data. Clarior only runs read-only queries against [SAP Business One](https://www.sap.com/products/erp/business-one.html), enforced by a read-only database user.

**Will conversational BI replace our BI dashboards?**
Usually not. Conversational BI suits everyday questions, and dashboards suit fixed, repeated reporting. Many teams save their best conversational answers as dashboards.
